You Lose What You Shoot

Original short film · Suspense

The story
A camera makes anything it photographs disappear from reality. The discovery of its power leads to a struggle for possession, with increasingly irreversible consequences. The film turns an everyday object into the centre of a suspenseful story about loss and responsibility.
My contribution
I developed the concept, story and ending, and brought the idea to screen through filming and editing. I combined disappearance effects with character reactions and shot sequencing to communicate the camera’s power through action.
The outcome
My first completed original short film, taking a creative idea from concept to a finished narrative and developing my skills in visual storytelling, production and post-production.

The Proposal

Original comedy short · Two teaser edits

The story
What looks like a romantic marriage proposal turns out to be a request to break up. The couple responds with unexpected enthusiasm, combining the gestures and emotional language of a proposal with brutally honest reasons for ending their relationship.
My contribution
I developed the concept and dialogue, organised filming and edited the finished video. I used pacing, character reactions and the contrast between romantic presentation and unexpected dialogue to build the reveal. I also created two teaser edits that introduced different clues about the story.
The outcome
A completed comedy short with supporting teaser content, demonstrating how a familiar social ritual can become an original story through humour and a shift in audience expectations.

About

I'm a marketer based in Barcelona who works with a camera and a drawing tablet. I hold a Master's in Marketing from EAE Business School and UPC.

From 2024 to 2025 I coordinated twelve product launches and the multilingual marketing materials behind them across six Latin American markets. That work showed me how images do their job inside a brand: on a brochure, at a trade-show stand, in a distributor presentation.

My own work is about light and small moments in the city. I'm looking for roles in content and brand marketing, and I take on photography and illustration projects.
